# AI Stack Audit

A free spreadsheet template that shows you what your AI subscription stack really costs you each month — and which tools are actually worth keeping.

🔗 **Live page:** _https://carminelaluna.github.io/ai-stack-audit/_
⬇️ **Direct download:** [ai-stack-audit-en.xlsx](./ai-stack-audit-en.xlsx)

## Why

Most solo founders and indie builders end up with 5-10 AI subscriptions (coding agents, notetakers, ad generators, infra tools...) and no single view of what it's costing them, or which ones are pulling their weight. This template gives you that view in under 10 minutes — no software, no sign-up, no tracking tool required.

## What's inside

The workbook has three sheets:

- **Quick guide** — how to fill it in and how to read the results.
- **AI Stack** — one row per tool: cost, category, usage frequency, perceived value. A formula scores each row and suggests **Keep / Review / Cut**.
- **Summary** — total monthly and annual spend, how many tools are cut candidates, and a chart of spend by category.

Works in Excel, Google Sheets, and Numbers.

## How to use it

1. Download [`ai-stack-audit-en.xlsx`](./ai-stack-audit-en.xlsx).
2. Replace the example row in the **AI Stack** sheet with your own subscriptions.
3. Rate each one on usage (1-5) and perceived value (1-5).
4. Check the **Summary** sheet for your totals and cut candidates.
